### 1.4 What production reaches at this range

The schema-4 engine is not reachable from the shipped binary at this range (`range/production-reach-of-the-topology-engine.log`):
- `src/topology/schema.rs` pins `TOPOLOGY_ACTIVATION` to `Inactive` with a compile-time assertion.
- `engine::topology` is `pub(crate)` under `cfg_attr(not(test), allow(dead_code))`.
- `create_run`, `run_recovery_order` and `startup_census` have no caller outside tests.

So every recovery this report credits ran **through the test suite's calls of the engine's own entry points**
(`TopologyRun::resumed` → `run_recovery_order`, `census_run_dirs`, `establish_stable_prefix`, the creator's error
path). The process funnel, its reaper and its kill paths are reachable in production through `HostRunner`, which the
legacy engine uses.

The same log records two narrower facts this report relies on:
- **The attempt-scope recoveries are unreachable.** `AttemptContext::settle_interrupted` is called outside tests only
  by `cancel_in_flight`, and `complete_promotion` only by `promote`; neither caller has a caller outside tests.
  `run_recovery_order` settles interrupted attempts through its own `recover::settle_interrupted` (§8.3).
- **`upstroke answer` cannot find a schema-4 question at this range.** The question-payload funnel is called outside
  tests only by `interaction::write_question`, and only the legacy coordinator and resume call that. The answer
  command finds questions by those payloads.
