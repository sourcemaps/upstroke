## 3. Readings taken on packet ambiguities

Each is a place where the packet, the errata and the shipped vocabulary together do not determine
the answer. The reading, the alternative, and why. Readings 1–9 are the handover brief's; each
was re-derived here from the packet and the code before being kept, and the ones marked
*(changed)* differ from the brief.

### R1 — closure steps (2)–(4) at max_parallel = 1 are refusals naming PR11

`closure_procedure` steps (2) (in-flight cancellation or drain), (3) (complete promotions) and (4)
(complete authorized publications) describe work the synchronous loop never leaves open when it
reaches closure: an attempt is settled inside the step that started it, a promotion completes
inside `promote_candidate`, and a transaction reaches its terminal inside `integrate`. A fresh
process's recovery steps (d)–(f) settle, promote and complete them before the loop runs.
**Reading:** `closure::refuse_unclosable` refuses those three shapes before any append, naming
`tokio_boundary` ("closure under concurrency (halt cancellation, budget drain with halting
conversion, in-flight cancellation on an append error)" — PR11's). Halting conversion during a
budget drain has no in-flight settlement to convert at this width; `closure::ending_outcome`
reads halt before budget, which is the same precedence. The refusal is a checkpoint refusal of
the kind `checkpoint_refusals` licenses; it is reachable only by a log no sequential process
writes. Until round 4 this said a test constructs one to hold the refusal, and the only test
that named `unclosable` was a positive fixture (`assert!(closure::unclosable(fold).is_empty())`,
the round-4 contract lens, F2); since round 4
`closure_refuses_an_in_flight_generation_and_an_unresolved_transaction_before_any_append`
replays two such logs — an attempt started and never settled, a verification started and never
settled — and holds `refuse_unclosable` to name each shape, PR11 and "nothing was appended";
`close_run` consults it after the ending outcome and before its first append. The promoting
shape has no fixture.

### R2 — the hard block falls through to closure

`loop`: "else apply the hard-block rules (attached-terminal prompt or wait_on_block for open
questions) -> else run-end closure". Every `AnswerSource` that yields `Unanswered` from `resolve`
(unattended, an exhausted `wait_on_block`, an empty terminal line) means nobody answered.
**Reading:** `hard_block` calls `close_run`, which derives Parked (no admissible work, no backoff,
questions open). `Progress::Blocked` is gone; `Progress::Finished` replaces it. A parked run
resumes through `run_resumed` alone, which the fold admits (`check_started_run`), and the next
incarnation's first step ingests an answer left in the run directory (`T-ANSWER`).

### R3 — the ending outcome is computed before the open generations are closed

`closure::ending_outcome`: halt > budget > the fold's derived outcome. Under halt or budget it is
computed *before* closing the open generations, because `derived_outcome` is NotEnding while an
OpenNoAttempt or RetainedIdle generation blocks `common`. This is `PR7-R4-LOOP-004`'s first
shape: closure closes first and re-derives (`closure::confirm_derived`), and a residual
NotEnding refusal names every blocker (`closure::blockers`) — the diagnostic that finding owed.
`GenerationCloseReason::RunEnding { outcome }` carries the outcome the closure is ending with.
Parked and Complete have no open generation by construction: the selector would have continued
or retried it, or the lineage's question would have been refused while it was open
(`check_question_can_park_lineage`).

### R4 — `Step::NotStarted` and `Step::Finished`

The selector no longer offers a closure for an unstarted fold (there is nothing to close and no
outcome to derive), and a fold whose `finished()` is set selects `Step::Finished(outcome)`. Both
are refused at the checkpoint before any append: "the run has not started" and "already finished
as … continuation of a finished run is refused after finalization". A parked or budget-stopped
run continues only through a resume, which the fold admits by `run_resumed` alone. `Admitted`
carries `Closure` and neither of the two; `LoopBranch::of` maps both to no branch.

### R5 — `run_finished.merged` and `.parked` are derived and not validated

`RunFinished4 { merged, parked }` are derived from the fold (tasks `Merged`; tasks
`AwaitingInput`) by `report::merged_and_parked`, and are **not** validated by
`check_run_finished`, whose pass rule reads `outcome` and `halted_at` only. Validating them would
be a Class B change to `src/topology/fold/check_end.rs`. Live and replay agree because both read
the fold. Recorded as a reading, not a gap: the derived-outcome guard is what INV-15 names, and
the counts are the report's convenience (`PR31-RUN-FINISHED-COUNTS-NEVER-CHECKED` stands as
filed).

### R6 — the report lists retained candidates refs for every outcome but Complete

`terminal_finalization` (v): candidates refs are pruned at Complete only and "retained and listed
at Halted". The report is written after `run_finished` and before the cleanup steps, so at
Complete it already describes the pruned state (no retained refs listed), and at Halted, Parked
and BudgetExceeded it lists every candidate's ref with its commit. `report.json` is "regenerated,
never read back": the only read is the digest comparison that decides whether to rewrite it
(`TopologyReport::is_fresh_against`), and a report that does not parse is stale.

### R7 — a resume recreates a pruned execution root

R18 is "pruned unless a later resume recreates it". `run_recovery_order` recreates it through
`Worktree.CreateExecutionRoot` when absent, before `reclaim_stale_residue`, with a warning. That
site's declared adjacency is `Before(run_started)` and its fault row `T-RUNSTART`; executing it
inside `T-RESUME` is this reading. The pinned registry (`effects/sequential-registry.json`)
carries the site's two hook-phase entries, keyed by site, phase and order and carrying the site's
own row, each witnessed by
`a_budget_stopped_run_with_a_retained_generation_is_closed_run_ending_and_ends` — the
resume-time execution, which is the one the sequential suites perform. The registry keys an
entry by its coordinate, not by the prefix that executed it, so there is no separate
"resume-time entry" beside a fresh-run one; the reviewed record said there was, when the
registry then carried no entry for the site at all, and that sentence is withdrawn (§13).

### R8 — the live retry path's Close arm scrubs the closed generation

`G4B-O3-LIVE-WORKTREE-MISSING-CLOSE-KEEPS-THE-INTENT`: after the `generation_closed` append
succeeds, `retry_ready`'s `Close` arm removes the closed generation's worktree (when residue, not
absence, failed the verification) and its durable intent through the workspace manager, as
`reclaim_closed_generations` does on resume. The test carries the intents read `G4G` made.

### R9 — the production reader stays at schema 3

`report::topology_status` and `TopologyReport::render` are test-reachable; `status::load` is
untouched. The ST-20 status half ("report.json and status name the run's kind, policy, image
reference, id, and digest") is asserted through them, and `contract_mappings.rs`'s `defer:PR10`
row maps to that test.

### R10 — closure at BudgetExceeded scrubs the closed generations' worktrees

`terminal_finalization`: "Parked and BudgetExceeded runs finalize only terminal items ((ii),
(iii), (iv)) and keep everything resumably_open"; R9's lifecycle: "OpenNoAttempt … closed at run
end", "candidate created or Closed: pruned (forced); intent removed". **Reading:** the worktree
of a generation *closure closes* is a Closed generation's and is pruned with the close, at
BudgetExceeded as at Halted, by `close_run` and not by the finalization step (i) — which at
BudgetExceeded and Parked does not run. Nothing resumably_open is touched: a resume dispatches a
fresh generation at the then-current head (ST-11), never the closed one.
